01 The sponsor thesis

Acquisition builds scale. Organic growth builds value.

Sponsors have put significant capital into RIA platforms. Market returns and tuck-in deals grow AUM, but the next buyer will ask a harder question: can this platform win new clients on its own?

i.

Buyers pay for proven growth

Exit buyers look for net-new assets that come from the platform itself, not from markets or acquisitions. Without a documented growth engine, that story is hard to tell.

ii.

Advisors aren't marketers

Acquired advisors are excellent with clients and rarely run disciplined prospecting. Growth depends on a handful of producers and is hard to repeat across the platform.

iii.

Fragmented vendors hide the data

Mailers, events, CRM, payments and referrals sit with different vendors. The sponsor can't see cost per household or return by market, so capital can't be allocated well.
